# Sign in

Open the app and you will see **Smart Bookmark Manager** with email and password fields.

## Create an account (email)

1. Click **Sign up**.
2. Enter your email and a password (at least 6 characters).
3. Click **Create account**.
4. Click **Sign in**, enter the same email and password, then click **Sign in**.

If sign-in says the email is not confirmed, your project still requires a confirmation email. Open the link in that email, then try **Sign in** again. On a local test setup, the person who runs the app can turn **Confirm email** off in Supabase so you can sign in immediately.

## Sign in (email)

1. Enter the email and password you used to register.
2. Click **Sign in**.

## Continue with Google

If you see **Continue with Google**:

1. Click it and choose your Google account.
2. Allow access if Google asks.
3. You should return to your workspace.

If you do **not** see that button, Google sign-in is not enabled for this install. Use email instead.

If Google shows a JSON error such as `provider is not enabled` or `missing OAuth secret`, Google is not fully configured. Use email until the person who runs the app finishes Google setup.

## Sign out

In the workspace header, click the **sign out** icon (arrow leaving a door) at the top right.

## After you sign in

You go to your workspace. Next: [Your workspace](workspace.md).
